Concept A · the built one, corrected

The proof sheet

What is committed as v54, with your three faults fixed: no “The Plates” heading, numerals centred under their frames, and the pictures now drift against the scroll inside their own windows — the frames never move, so nothing on the page shifts. Hover still dims the sheet and lights one plate.

MotionScroll parallax inside a fixed frame, plus the once-only rise. CostTwo CSS rules and one rAF read. No new images, no new observer. RiskNone. It is the shipped object with its faults removed.

Concept B · vertical scroll, horizontal travel

The ribbon

The section pins to the window and your downward scroll pulls the ten plates sideways past you. Tall crops — 2:3, not 3:2 — which is the honest answer to “vertical or horizontal”: the plates stand vertical so the strip can run horizontal without slivers. The plate at centre lights up and names itself; the rest hold back.

MotionScroll-scrubbed travel. Every frame counter-parallaxes its own picture. Cost340vh of page height before chapter I, and all ten images want loading. RiskThe pin fights the dawn-arc paper lerp in js/motion.js. Needs a real test.

Concept C · the awwwards reference, made a book object

The list

This is the Mario Roudil transition you sent. Ten rows of type, separated by rules — no cards, no grid, no pictures on the page at rest. Point at a row and its plate unrolls out of the rule itself as a letterbox band while the other nine fade back. It is the quietest at rest and the most expensive-looking in motion.

MotionPointer-driven clip-path unroll, 520ms, plus a slow scale settle. CostSmallest of the four at rest — nothing renders until asked for. RiskIt is a table of contents, and the site already has one below it.

Concept D · five columns, five speeds

The wall

Not a grid of equal cells. Five columns, offset vertically and drifting at five slightly different rates, so the field breathes as you pass it. Frames alternate portrait and square. Paper between them instead of hairlines — deliberately a different object from A, and the most designed of the four.

MotionTrue per-column parallax. Titles are always visible; nothing hides. CostTallest section of the four, and it will not compress to a phone as one object. RiskThe one furthest from “a book”. It reads gallery, not proof sheet.
