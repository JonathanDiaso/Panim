Accessibility

This page says what the site aims for, what has actually been measured, what has not been done yet, and how to tell me when something is broken for you.

What this site aims for

PANIM aims to meet WCAG 2.2 Level AA. That is the standard this site is built and checked against.

This is a statement of intent and of current status, not a claim of full conformance. Where the site falls short of AA today, it is listed below rather than left out.

What has been measured

These are done and were checked with numbers rather than by eye:

  • Text contrast. The three ink tiers run 15.5:1, 6.4:1 and 4.5:1 against the darkest paper stock in the book, so they clear AA against all ten stocks, not just the warm ones.
  • The Listening Room's dim states were raised off 3.2:1 and 2.0:1, both of which were failures carrying real text.
  • Tap targets are at least 44×44 px.
  • The smallest type on the site is 10.24 px and is only ever used for tracked-out uppercase. Nothing renders under 10 px; eleven declarations used to.
  • Every accent colour in the palette was measured against its own paper stock before it shipped.
  • Motion respects prefers-reduced-motion.

What has not been done yet

Being straight about this is the point of the page. None of the following has had a full pass:

  • Every image's alt text judged rather than counted — confirming each one says what the picture is doing, not just that a string is present.
  • Keyboard navigation walked end to end, including every trap and every skip.
  • Focus order and focus visibility inside every modal and sheet.
  • The audio player and the Listening Room driven with a screen reader.
  • Every player state — playing, loading, error, sleep — checked for whether it is actually announced.

This work is scheduled. Until it is finished, assume there are real problems in those areas.

Known limitations

  • The book is a long single reading surface. It is navigable by keyboard, but a screen reader user has not yet confirmed the experience end to end.
  • Audio is the primary medium here. Every chapter has a full read-along text on the page, so the words are available without the audio — but the recordings are not separately captioned or transcribed as timed text.

Telling me something is broken

If any part of this site does not work for you, I want to know, and a specific report is worth more than a general one. Useful details: the page, what you were trying to do, what happened instead, and the browser and assistive technology you were using.

lockstepusa@gmail.com

I read every one of these and I will tell you what I am able to fix and when.

How this site is tested

  • Contrast ratios calculated against each chapter's own paper stock, not against a single reference background.
  • Layout and type sizes measured at a 402 px viewport — a real phone width, not a desktop window narrowed down.
  • A full WCAG 2.2 AA audit, finding by finding, is in progress; this page will be updated with its results rather than replaced.
