# The three direction studies — 2026-08-27

Three complete visual directions, built as standalone pages before any of the site
was rebuilt. **B won.** The site you are looking at is Direction B, and the long note
at the top of `css/site.css` is that decision written down.

| | direction | the idea | why it did or did not win |
|---|---|---|---|
| `a.html` | **The Sheet** | Instrument Serif + Newsreader on bone paper, one cold blue, twelve columns, nothing centred. The Hebrew word set enormous as the opening image. | Closest runner-up. The asymmetric grid and the open outer column survived into B; the display face and the blue did not. |
| `b.html` | **The Plate** | Fraunces on a warm gallery wall, one clay accent taken from the pots. **The photograph gets the whole page and no type is ever laid on top of it.** | **Shipped.** Rule 2 in `css/site.css` — *a photograph is never a backdrop* — is this page's argument, and it is what makes the photography look expensive. Fraunces was later replaced by Literata over its italic; see `fonts/fonts.css`. |
| `c.html` | **The Terminal** | True black, JetBrains Mono chrome, one hot red signal used four times, hard rules, no blur. | Lost, but not wasted: it is the night palette, and it is the reference if the Listening Room or a dark mode is ever built out. `css/room.css` is the nearest thing to it that shipped. |

They sat untracked in the site root as `_dir/` for a day, which is why they are here
instead — kept, because the argument is worth keeping, and filed, because the site
root is not a scratch folder.
