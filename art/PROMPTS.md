# PANIM — image prompts

Two prompts per picture. Generate **1**, then send **2** on the same image. The second
one is usually the keeper — the model's first answer is always too pretty, too full and
too evenly lit, and **2** takes things away.

---

## The one thing that was wrong before

The old sheet opened every prompt with the same line — *golden hour, god rays, warm amber.*
Six of eleven pictures were ordered in identical light. That is why the set came back
samey, and it fights the book: the site's paper runs **night to morning** across the ten
chapters, and if every plate is golden the arc dies in the pictures.

**What stays constant is the optics.** Backlight. Real haze. Blacks that stay black.
Depth in layers. Bloom on the brightest edge only.

**What moves is the light.** Every chapter already has a colour on the page. The plate
takes its light from that:

| ch | the page | the light to ask for |
|---|---|---|
| I | rust | one hard shaft into a dark room |
| II | cold blue | dusk, twenty minutes after sundown, no warmth left |
| III | ember | night; firelight inside smoke is the only source |
| IV | night water | deepest night, moon on water, one thin line of dawn |
| V | flame | the fire is the only light and it falls off fast |
| VI | gold | hard low sun directly behind, blown at the rim |
| VII | slate | overcast, colour draining out |
| VIII | blood | storm-dark, red-brown, one rim of light |
| IX | amber | first grey light, plus one small warm fire |
| X | morning | full clean sun, no haze at all |

---

## How to write the second prompt

**Subtract.** Darker, emptier, closer, colder, further back. Name one thing to remove and
one thing to keep. Never add a new element in **2** — you will get a new picture instead
of a better one.

Four words that do most of the work: *darker · emptier · closer · further back.*

**Never ask for "photorealistic" or "not an illustration."** It flattens the light.
**Never stack adjectives.** Say the light source, its direction and its colour once, say
what things are made of, and say in one clause what the picture is about.

---

## The set

Deliver **3:2 landscape, 2400 px or better, JPEG**. Name by slot — `ch07-gate.jpg`.
Keep the subject **centred**: sides crop on desktop, top and bottom on phones.

**No full face until chapter X.** Hidden, turned, veiled, backlit, seen from behind, too
far away to read. Chapter X is the first face in full light and it only lands if it is
the first.

### `hero` — the door · *have one, reroll optional*

> **1.** A heavy olivewood door standing half open in a windowless stone room. The only light in the picture comes through the gap: low sun, warm, landing on the floor as one hard-edged wedge with dust moving in it. The far corners of the room stay black. Shot from inside at standing height, 35mm, the door slightly off centre. The picture is about being invited in, not about the door.

> **2.** Same room, same door. Darker, and the wedge of light narrower and harder — one shape on the floor, not a glow filling the space. Remove any second light source. Let the far wall go completely black.

---

### `ch01-tomb` — Chamber 25 · *have one; reroll if the anachronisms bother you*

> **1.** Looking straight down through a broken slab in an ancient limestone floor into a burial chamber below. A boy's dusty hand grips the jagged edge at the top of frame. Below: clay oil lamps, plain undecorated pottery, undisturbed dust. One shaft of afternoon sun comes through the break and is the only light reaching the chamber. 35mm, directly overhead. Iron Age Judah, 600 BC — nothing metal, nothing written, nothing later than that.

> **2.** Same shot, darker. Let two thirds of the frame go to black. Keep the hand, the broken edge, the shaft of light and three or four clay lamps. Remove everything else.

---

### `ch01-scroll` — the oldest words · *have one*

> **1.** Extreme close-up of a small rolled strip of silver, blackened and cracked with age, lying in an open palm. It is the size of a cigarette. Faint scratched Hebrew letters catch a low raking light across the metal. The hand is out of focus behind it. 135mm macro, the scroll filling the frame.

> **2.** Same object, closer. Fill the frame with the silver alone, the palm only a soft field behind it. Rake the light lower until the scratched letters are the brightest thing in the picture and everything else is dull grey metal.

---

### `ch02-trees` — the hiding · **reroll — this is the priority**

The published one is golden and lush. Chapter II is the coldest page in the book.

> **1.** A garden at dusk, twenty minutes after the sun has gone. The colour has drained out of everything — blue-grey leaves, cold shadow, no warm light anywhere in the frame. Two figures in rough linen press themselves against the far side of two tree trunks, backs to us, faces hidden by the trunks. Deeper in, between the trees, a soft pale light is moving toward them. 85mm, waist height, the figures small and off to one side. The picture is about being looked for.

> **2.** Same garden, same two figures. Colder and emptier: remove the flowers and the undergrowth detail, let the ground go dark. Keep only the two trunks, the two hidden figures and the pale light between them. No golden light anywhere.

---

### `ch02-storm` — hurled · **needed**

> **1.** A small wooden merchant ship in open sea at night, shot broadside from water level as a wave lifts under it. Rain in sheets. The only light is a cold blue-white break in the cloud behind, which turns the water to moving glass and the ship to a black shape. Cargo going over the side. 35mm, low. The sea should look aimed at this one ship.

> **2.** Same frame, harder. Remove the sailors. Remove any warm lantern light. Push the ship smaller and further off centre, and let the foreground wave take a third of the picture. Cold blue-white only.

---

### `ch03-mountain` — the mountain burns · *have one*

> **1.** A desert mountain at night, its summit inside a column of smoke lit from within by fire, lightning inside the smoke, ash falling through the dark. Far below and far back a crowd stands at the edge of the plain facing it, not moving closer — small enough to read as a line rather than as people. 24mm, low, looking up. Firelight is the only colour; everything else is black and ash-grey.

> **2.** Same mountain. Push the crowd further back and smaller. Kill any moonlight so the fire inside the smoke is the single source. More smoke, less rock.

---

### `ch04-river` — the night at the ford · *have one*

> **1.** Two men locked together wrestling in shin-deep river water at the darkest part of the night. Water level, 50mm, close enough that spray is in the foreground. Moonlight behind them turns the thrown water white and both men into black shapes with a hard thin rim. No faces. A single band of grey at the horizon — dawn, still hours off. Deep blue-black everywhere else.

> **2.** Same two figures, later in the fight. Camera almost in the water. Thin the rim light further. They should look exhausted rather than athletic — heavier, lower, leaning into each other instead of grappling.

---

### `ch05-bush` — not consumed · *have one*

> **1.** A desert thorn bush on bare rock burning with a steady flame that is not eating it — the branches inside the fire are still green and whole. The fire is the only light in the picture and it falls off fast, so the desert around it is dark. A pair of worn leather sandals set down on the stone in the near foreground, catching the edge of the firelight. 50mm, low, close to the ground.

> **2.** Same bush. Darken everything the fire does not reach until the picture is mostly black. Keep the green inside the flame clearly visible — that is the whole point of the picture. Move the sandals further forward, half in shadow.

---

### `ch06-shine` — borrowed light · *have one*

> **1.** A head and shoulders completely covered by heavy woven cloth, shot against a low sun directly behind. The sun sits right at the edge of the fabric so the weave glows and loose threads light up like filament. No face, no eyes, no shape of features. Hard gold, blown out at the rim, deep shadow on the near side. 85mm, eye level, tight.

> **2.** Same figure. Overexpose the light behind until the top edge of the cloth is pure white and the weave only survives lower down. Nothing else in frame — no landscape, no second figure. The light must read as borrowed: clearly coming from behind, never from the cloth.

---

### `ch07-gate` — the glory backs out · **needed**

> **1.** The inside of an enormous empty stone temple hall, shot straight down its length toward a tall open doorway at the far end. Overcast daylight outside — grey, colourless, no sun. The light is brightest at the threshold and gets thinner and greyer as it comes toward us, so where we stand it is nearly gone. Dust in still air. 24mm, eye level, one-point perspective, dead centre. No people, no statues, no carving. The picture is about something having just left.

> **2.** Same hall, emptier. Strip all decoration from the stone. Drain the colour until it is close to monochrome slate. Push the doorway further away and darken the near half of the hall. Nothing warm anywhere in the frame.

---

### `ch08-flint` — set like flint · **needed** *(chapter VIII's title plate)*

> **1.** A man's profile turned hard to one side against a storm-dark sky. 135mm, tight — nose, lip, jaw and the line of the throat, nothing else. One low red-brown light rakes the edge of the profile and dies immediately; the rest is near black. Stubble and loose hair catch as filament. First-century Middle Eastern, no crown, no blood, no halo. The expression is not anguish. It is a decision already made.

> **2.** Same profile, tighter and darker. Crop until the eye is out of frame or lost in shadow. Reduce the rim light until it traces only the nose and jaw. Flat storm-grey behind, no detail at all.

---

### `ch08-veil` — torn · **needed**

> **1.** A colossal woven temple curtain, floor to ceiling, splitting from the top downward. Shot from below at 24mm so the fabric towers. Hard white light bursts through the widening tear; individual threads snap and light up. The heavy fabric is still swinging. Blue, purple and scarlet in the weave, most of it blown out near the tear. No people, no hands, no figure behind it.

> **2.** Same curtain one second later — the tear longer, reaching further down toward us. More light through the split, less visible fabric. Let the top third of the picture go to pure white. Keep the snapping threads.

---

### `ch09-emmaus` — the table after · **needed** *(chapter IX's title plate)*

> **1.** A rough wooden table in a dim stone room, shot from table height toward a small window. 35mm. On it: a round flatbread freshly torn in half, a clay cup, a low chair pushed back and empty. First grey light outside the window; one small oil lamp is the only warm thing in the picture. A thin line of smoke still rising from a wick just gone out. No people. Someone left this table half a second ago.

> **2.** Same table. Darker room, brighter window, so the bread and cup are almost silhouettes with a warm edge from the lamp. Clear everything else off the table. Keep the smoke line — it should be the sharpest detail in the frame.

---

### `ch09-charcoal` — the second fire · **needed**

> **1.** A small charcoal fire burning by itself on a grey pebble beach at first light. 50mm, camera almost on the stones. Low smoke drifts sideways and is backlit into a thin sheet. Two fish and flat bread on the coals. Wet sand, bare footprints leading toward the fire and stopping. Cold grey sea and sky behind; the fire is the only warm colour and it is small. No people, no boats, no birds.

> **2.** Same fire, camera lower still, until the smoke crosses the whole frame. Cool the sea and sky further toward grey-blue and keep the fire small. It should look like a fire someone built for you and then stepped away from.

---

### `ch10-morning` — face to face · *have one*

> **1.** A woman's face in full clean morning sunlight. 85mm, eye level, close. Eyes open, looking straight at the camera. Not smiling — steady, the look of someone who has stopped bracing. Tears at the lower lid, not falling. Direct bright light with no haze at all; skin, lashes and the edge of the iris are sharp. Plain warm background with nothing to read.

> **2.** Same face, same light, closer — eyes and mouth fill the frame. Remove the background entirely to soft warm white. Keep the sharpness. This is the only picture in the set allowed to be clear.

---

### `ch10-veil-lift` — she lifts her eyes · **needed**

> **1.** A woman on a camel at the edge of a field in the evening, seen from the side and at a distance. 24mm, low, small in frame. She has lifted a light veil away from her face with one hand and is looking across the field. Far across it a single man walks toward her. Warm low sun behind them both, long shadows, dry grass. Neither face is readable at this distance. The picture is about two people seeing each other in the same moment.

> **2.** Same field, same two. Pull back further so they are smaller and the field is most of the picture. Warmer, later light — almost the last minute of sun. Keep both of them and both long shadows reaching toward each other.

---

### `og-image` — the social card

Not a new picture. Crop `hero` or `ch10-morning` to **1200 × 630**, subject left of centre,
and leave the right third quiet — that is where the link title lands.

## Shot map — so the set has rhythm

Ten well-lit mid-shots is still ten mid-shots. As ordered:

```
hero            wide 35     ch05-bush       medium 50     ch09-emmaus     medium 35
ch01-tomb       overhead 35 ch06-shine      close 85      ch09-charcoal   low 50
ch01-scroll     macro 135   ch07-gate       wide 24       ch10-morning    close 85
ch02-trees      medium 85   ch08-flint      macro 135     ch10-veil-lift  wide 24
ch02-storm      wide 35     ch08-veil       wide 24
ch03-mountain   wide 24
ch04-river      medium 50
```

If a reroll comes back good but the set still feels flat, change the **shot**, not the
light. The light is doing the arc.
