Let me see your face; let me hear your voice.

Song of Songs 2:14

There is one Hebrew word for face. It is on nearly every page of the Bible, and almost nobody sees it.

I followed it from the first page to the last. It changed what I thought the book was about, and it changed what I thought God had been asking me for the whole time.

I wrote it, and that is me reading it: ten chapters, start to finish. Read along, or close your eyes and just listen. It keeps your place either way.

About this book

PANIM traces the Hebrew word for face, panim, across the whole of Scripture.

Written and narrated by Jonathan Diaso. Ten chapters, five and a half hours, free. Your place is saved on this device.

Thank you for giving it your evening. If any of it landed, the only thing I would ask is that you send it to one person you thought of while you were listening.
