The invitation hidden on every page

Panim is the Hebrew word for face. Counting its compound forms it appears more than two thousand times in the Bible — which means the face of God has been sitting inside sentences you have read all your life, hidden under a word as ordinary as this one.

This book follows that single word from the first page of Scripture to the last: a garden where two people hide, a river at midnight, a mountain on fire, a curtain torn from the top down, and a supper table the night before a death.

Between those two pages the face hides, shines, bleeds and comes home. It is a love story, the oldest one still running — and before it is over you will find your own face in it.
