The invitation hidden on every page

Panim is the Hebrew word for face. It is in your Bible more than two thousand times, hidden under a word as ordinary as this one.

Between the first page and the last the face hides, shines, bleeds and comes home. It is a love story — the oldest one still running — and before it is over you will find your own face in it.

About this book

PANIM traces the Hebrew word for face — panim — across the whole of Scripture: a single word, followed from the first page to the last, until it becomes the whole story.
